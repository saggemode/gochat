# Delete your GoChat account & data

_Operated by: [YOUR COMPANY/NAME]_ · _Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]_

> **Google Play requires a publicly reachable URL that explains this.** Host this page (e.g.
> `https://[yourdomain]/delete-account`) and enter it in Play Console → App content → Data deletion.

## Delete in the app (fastest)
1. Open GoChat → **Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete my account**.
3. Confirm twice. Your account and data are removed and you're signed out immediately.

## Delete by request
If you can't access the app, email **[SUPPORT EMAIL]** from your registered contact, subject
"Delete my account", including your GoChat PIN or phone number. We verify and process within 30 days.

## What gets deleted
Your profile, messages you sent, your stories, marketplace listings/orders, contacts, and device/push
tokens are permanently removed from our active systems. Encrypted backups are purged on their normal
rotation cycle (within [30] days).

## What may be retained
Minimal records we're legally required to keep (e.g. transaction/tax records for completed
marketplace orders) are retained for the period required by law, then deleted. Content you sent to
other users may remain in **their** copy of the conversation.
