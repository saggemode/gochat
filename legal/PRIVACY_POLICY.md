## 1. Who we are
GoChat ("we", "us") provides a messaging and marketplace app. This policy explains what we collect,
why, and your choices.

## 2. Information we collect
- **Account data:** phone number, display name, profile photo, and a generated PIN.
- **Content you create:** messages, media (images/video/voice/documents), stories, and marketplace
  listings/orders. Messages may be end-to-end encrypted when you enable that option.
- **Contacts:** if you allow contact sync, phone numbers from your address book are matched to GoChat
  users so you can find people. We store which matched users are your contacts.
- **Usage & device data:** app version, device model, OS, language, and diagnostic/crash data
  (via Firebase Crashlytics) to keep the app working.
- **Push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)** to deliver notifications.
- **Approximate/precise location** only when you share a location in chat (not continuous tracking).

## 3. How we use it
To provide messaging/calls, the marketplace, notifications, security (login, anti-abuse), and to
diagnose crashes. We do **not** sell your personal data.

## 4. Sharing
With other users as part of normal app use (e.g. the people you chat with). With service providers
that host our systems: **Supabase** (database/storage), **Render** (servers), **Google Firebase**
(push, crash reporting), and the provider used for media/CDN. Each processes data on our behalf.

## 5. Payments
Marketplace payments are processed by [PAYMENT PROVIDER, e.g. Paystack/Flutterwave]. We do not store
your full card details; the provider handles them under its own policy.

## 6. Data retention & deletion
We keep your data while your account is active. You can **delete your account in the app**
(Settings → Delete my account), or request deletion at [SUPPORT EMAIL]. Deletion removes your
profile, messages, orders, and stories from our systems (backups are purged on their rotation cycle).
See the separate [Account & Data Deletion](ACCOUNT_DELETION.md) page.

## 7. Security
Transport is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS). Optional end-to-end encryption is available for direct chats.
No method is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

## 8. Children
GoChat is not intended for anyone under [13/16 per your market]. We don't knowingly collect their data.

## 9. Your rights
Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR/NDPR), you may access, correct, export, or delete your data.
Contact [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## 10. Changes
We'll update this page and the "Last updated" date when this policy changes.
